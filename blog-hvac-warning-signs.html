Seven signs your HVAC system needs professional attention

Small performance changes can provide useful warning before a major comfort problem develops.

In this articleAirflow and temperatureSounds and smellsCycling and energy useSafety concerns

Heating and cooling systems often show subtle warning signs before they stop working. Paying attention early can protect comfort and may prevent additional strain on the equipment.

1. Weak airflow

Air that barely moves from supply vents may point to a dirty filter, blower problem, restriction, damaged ductwork, or another airflow concern.

2. Uneven temperatures

Persistent hot and cold rooms can be connected to airflow, insulation, duct leakage, controls, or equipment capacity.

3. New or unusual noises

Grinding, rattling, squealing, buzzing, and repeated clicking are useful clues. Turn the system off if a noise sounds electrical or severe.

4. Unusual odors

Dust at the start of a season can be temporary. Burning, electrical, gas, or persistent musty odors need prompt investigation.

5. Frequent cycling

A system that starts and stops repeatedly may have a control, airflow, sizing, or equipment issue.

6. Rising energy use

A noticeable increase without a major weather or usage change can signal lost efficiency or longer run times.

7. Water, ice, or safety concerns

Visible water, recurring ice, sparking, or burning smells should never be ignored. If you smell gas or suspect carbon monoxide, leave the building and contact the appropriate utility or emergency service.
