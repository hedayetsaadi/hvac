Airflow warning signs

Look beyond the equipment.

Hot and cold rooms

Persistent room-to-room differences may come from duct design or leakage.

Noisy airflow

Whistling, rattling, and rushing air can signal restrictions or loose sections.

High energy use

Conditioned air lost through leaks makes the system run longer than necessary.
