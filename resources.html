Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve clear answers.

How often should my HVAC system be serviced?

Most systems benefit from professional maintenance twice a year: cooling service in spring and heating service in fall. Equipment type, age, and usage may change that schedule.

When should I change the air filter?

Check it monthly. Many one-inch filters need replacement every one to three months, while thicker media filters may last longer. Pets, allergies, construction dust, and heavy use can shorten the interval.

Should I repair or replace an older system?

A repair may be practical when the system is otherwise reliable. Replacement becomes more attractive when breakdowns repeat, comfort is uneven, efficiency is poor, or a major repair approaches the value of newer equipment.

Why are some rooms warmer or colder?

Common causes include duct leakage, airflow restrictions, insulation gaps, equipment sizing, and thermostat location. A whole-system evaluation can identify the cause.

What can I check before calling?

Confirm the thermostat setting, replace a visibly dirty filter, check the circuit breaker, and make sure supply vents are open. Stop and call a professional if you smell gas, hear electrical arcing, or see ice or water near equipment.
