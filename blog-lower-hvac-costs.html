Simple ways to lower heating and cooling costs

Good airflow, consistent thermostat habits, and routine care can help equipment operate more effectively.

In this articleFilters and ventsThermostat habitsThe home envelopeMaintenance

Energy use depends on weather, equipment, insulation, airflow, thermostat settings, and how the home is used. Start with simple habits before assuming the system needs replacement.

Keep filters and vents clear

Check the filter monthly and replace it according to its condition and manufacturer guidance. Keep supply and return vents open and unobstructed by furniture, rugs, and curtains.

Use consistent thermostat settings

Large, frequent temperature changes can make comfort less predictable. A modest scheduled setback while the home is empty is usually easier to manage.

Reduce unwanted heat movement

Air sealing, attic insulation, shaded windows, and weather stripping can reduce the amount of heating or cooling the home needs.

Use ceiling fans correctly

Fans help people feel comfortable but do not cool empty rooms. Turn them off when a room is unoccupied.

Schedule seasonal maintenance

Clean components, proper airflow, and verified controls support more consistent operation. Maintenance can also identify developing problems before peak weather.

Still seeing unusually high bills?

A system and airflow evaluation can help identify where performance is being lost.
